# poof visual design

The visual design for the flow in `../wireframes/`, on the Poof styleguide (`poof-styleguide.html`).

- **`prototype.html`**: the clickable app prototype. Open it on a phone (full screen) or on desktop (one phone, "Restart" next to it). It starts on Home; "+ Sell" runs the whole story: snap, recognise, goal, minimum and goal price, handover, Poof at work, your ad, approve, platforms going live, pushes, chats (Tom's low offer is answered from quick replies in the chat, Mila's deal plays out by itself), and the sold poof with next steps.
- **`design-a.html`**: the chosen direction as a board (Overview, Play, `?figma` one-row export), including three options for the sold moment.
- **`design-b.html`**: the alternative, Mobbin-led direction (not chosen), kept for reference. Sources at the bottom of the page.
- **`SHIPPING.md`**: how this design gets into the live app safely.

Figma: [poof · visual design](https://www.figma.com/design/2nBiDNYGp5vcxwjGSPQXlm) (Todaytomorrow team): A, B, and "A, round 2".

Decisions baked in (Teije, 26 September 2026): primary button is ink with lime text; "Poof", never "agent"; cut-outs are stickers with real paper price tags; one active item in the navbar; no separate "Going live" or "Your call" screens (going live happens on the product page, a low offer is handled in the chat); sold is a poof moment, not a receipt.

Assets in `assets/`: cut-outs for overviews, original ad photos for product pages (the PS4 was cut out with macOS Vision subject lifting, the other items come from the Poof brandboard), platform logos in `assets/logos/` (Simple Icons for Vinted, eBay and Facebook, the official Marktplaats app icon), and the poof sprite and sounds from the waitlist page.

Demo items: AirPods (default), a Pioneer DDJ-400 DJ controller, an orange donut lamp, a shiatsu massage pillow and a padel racket. Pick the one you will photograph in the presenter sheet (triple-tap "Your ads"); if you forget, "Fix it" on "Is this it?" lets you switch. Each item has its own title, description, prices, market histogram, chats and cut-out. Sample photos: `assets/demo-1..4.jpg` (AirPods) and `assets/demo/` (the other items, resized from the originals in `assets/demo-products/`, which are not deployed). Cut-outs were made with macOS Vision subject lifting. The camera photos you take replace the sample photos; recognition, prices and buyers are scripted and nothing is sent anywhere.

Filming the demo: sell the item live (photo, 3 steps, approve, going live, Tom's offer in the chat), cut, then triple-tap "Your ads" and choose "Skip ahead: sold, a few days later". The sold push arrives; tap it for the poof moment, then "See the ad and chats" for the sold overview and every chat (Mila's deal, Tom, Noor).
